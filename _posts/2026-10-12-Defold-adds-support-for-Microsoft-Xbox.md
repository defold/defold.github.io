_The Defold Foundation is pleased to announce that the Defold game engine now officially supports game development for Microsoft Xbox consoles._

STOCKHOLM, SWEDEN - October 12, 2026 - The Defold Foundation today announced the availability of Microsoft Xbox development in Defold 1.14.0 for approved Xbox developers provided free of charge. This completes a major expansion of Defold's console capabilities, with the engine now supporting the three major console platform families: Microsoft Xbox, PlayStation®4 and PlayStation®5, and Nintendo Switch.

## Getting access to Xbox development

Developers must first be approved for Xbox console development. Read the [Microsoft game publishing page](https://developer.microsoft.com/en-US/games/publish) to learn about Microsoft's publishing programmes, including ID@Xbox, and complete the registration and approval process.

![ID@xbox](/images/posts/xbox/id_at_xbox.webp)

_ID@Xbox (Independent Developers @ Xbox) is a program by Microsoft that lets independent game developers self-publish digital games on Xbox consoles, Windows PC, and cloud gaming platforms._

Once Microsoft has approved your organisation, sign in to the Xbox middleware page using an account associated with it, find Defold, and request access.

After the request has been verified, the Defold Foundation will provide access to the Xbox-specific resources and instructions, including:

* the Xbox extension with platform integrations such as user management and game saves;
* information about the required development tools and the workflow for building, bundling, and testing Xbox games;
* access to Xbox-specific developer support.

The [Microsoft Xbox development manual](/manuals/microsoft-xbox/) contains the current access and setup instructions.

Xbox support is available from Defold 1.14.0. As with all console platforms, development requires additional tools that we cannot distribute due to NDA restrictions. Setup instructions are provided to approved developers through the channels described in the manual.

As with our other console integrations, access to platform-specific functionality and documentation is provided for approved developers for free, without any hidden fees.

##### About Defold

Defold is a free-to-use, source-available game engine with a developer-friendly license for creating cross-platform games. Developers use Defold to ship games on web, mobile, desktop, and consoles. Defold is known for its small runtime, focus on performance, fast workflows, and friendly community.

##### About Defold Foundation

The Defold Foundation is the owner of the Defold game engine. The foundation works together with industry partners and community to provide software and services to game developers across the globe. The foundation was created in May 2020 and is registered in Stockholm, Sweden.

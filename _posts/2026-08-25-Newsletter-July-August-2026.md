## News

Hello Defolder!

Welcome to our summer newsletter edition, covering both July and August!

**Defold 1.13.1 is out** with new **Light components** and many more improvements, the Defold Game Jam was announced and will be there soon, and we have a great collection of games, extensions, tools, articles, and videos from the community to show to you. If you have something you wish to share, please send us an email at [info@defold.se](mailto:info@defold.se).

## Defold Game Jam 2026

![Defold Game Jam 2026](/images/posts/newsletters/2026-august/game_jam_announcement.webp)

It is time for another [Defold Game Jam](https://itch.io/jam/defold-game-jam-2026)! Join us in September, make a game, meet fellow developers, enjoy online talks, and compete for prizes. Among announced sponsors we have us, the Defold Foundation, Wavedash and CraftPix - check the rewards and stay tuned, as there's still more to come!

## [Defold 1.13.1 has been released](https://defold.com/2026/08/17/Defold-1-13-1/)

![Defold 1.13.1 has been released](/images/posts/newsletters/2026-august/defold_1_13_1.webp)

Highlights:

- 💡 New Light components
- 💪 Instanced models with morph targets
- 🎮 SDL gamepad database
- ⚡ Up to 19% faster warm builds
- 🖥️ Borderless desktop fullscreen
- 🛠️ Expanded Editor Scripts and HTTP API
- 📦 Resource archives larger than 4 GiB
- 🔊 HTML5 audio fixes and more!

[CHECK THE RELEASE ANNOUNCEMENT](https://defold.com/2026/08/17/Defold-1-13-1/)
